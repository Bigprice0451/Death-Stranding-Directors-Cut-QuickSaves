## If the publisher includes NVIDIA binaries in a future ZIP

The starting point is the pinned base license plus the RTX supplement. Each addresses a different part of publication. [NVIDIA license and supplement](https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS/blob/v310.9.16/LICENSE.txt)

- Base §§1(c), 2(a), 2(c) and 4(b) allow distribution of eligible SDK material incorporated as object code in an application with material additional functionality. Downstream terms must protect NVIDIA's grants, restrictions and intellectual property at least as strongly as the license. The stand-alone SDK is expressly excluded. An integrated NGX graphics feature makes the object-code/application grant relevant. Keep the graphics component's end-user terms and NVIDIA notices together when applying this grant. Base §2(d) also sets reporting and enforcement duties for known or suspected noncompliance. [Base license](https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS/blob/v310.9.16/LICENSE.txt)
- Supplement §4 requires notice before **commercial release** of an application incorporating or based on the DLSS/NGX SDK, including a plug-in to a commercial application. The publisher must assess its release and make any required notification with the requested publication details. [NVIDIA notification page](https://developer.nvidia.com/sw-notification)
- Supplement §7.1(b) requires SDK attribution and the applicable NVIDIA marks on splash screens, in an about box when present, and in credits for game applications. §7.1(a) addresses identifying NVIDIA as the SDK supplier in specified marketing materials and calls for prior written approval of such references. §7.2(c) covers the permitted marks, use guidelines and submission of each proposed use at least two weeks before its desired implementation. Approval must be in writing. No response within ten business days counts as unapproved. This project records no publisher notification or mark approval. [RTX marketing terms](https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS/blob/v310.9.16/LICENSE.txt)
- Supplement §§1 and 6.2 cover NVIDIA GPU interoperability and public DLSS integrations with material technical issues. Base §4(c) addresses implied sponsorship or endorsement. Keep the relevant rights and obligations separate from the open-source terms for the save add-on's other dependencies.
